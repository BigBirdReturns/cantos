SecondRun / Post-release observation

What does the medal establish?

ClusterMAX 3.0 is released. This ledger records 17 selected provider assignments and the scope of the evidence reviewed. It is not a 77-provider audit or a customer-outcome result.

What the report actually tests

The inspected article describes managed clusters, configuration audits, compute/network/storage tests, training and inference workloads, extended load, and fault recovery. It also uses customer feedback and separates short hands-on observations from longer-run questions. The published testing is real evidence; this challenge asks a different, narrower question.

What our test adds

Hold the workload, ordinary customer treatment, baseline and mapping fixed before new jobs. Compare the baseline against the same predictor with the medal added, including a medal-blind reference adjustment. Retain all terminal attempts and report uncertainty across providers. No real prospective outcome packet has been submitted.

The report explains different recovery expectations for different hardware generations. A useful comparison therefore carries the actual service, hardware, permissions, support and observation period. A provider logo alone does not encode those conditions.

What remains unresolved in this review

The ledger records partial global scope and test conditions; exact provider-specific dates and service configurations have not been normalized. An out-of-sample comparison with a frozen specs-and-price baseline was not identified in the reviewed sections. Provider appendix entries are not individually adjudicated here. These are limits of this review, not claims that evidence exists nowhere.
