# Cantos reader-facing language

Cantos is **AI workflow software for continuing work across engineers, models and infrastructure**. Second Run is its execution-and-reuse capability. Introduce that relationship before component names, mechanisms or internal history.

The ordinary reading order is product, useful operation, available action, expected result, supporting evidence. Historical collections are a primary entry: measurements, configurations, issue histories, price snapshots, workload traces and research retain their original sources and conditions. Our own runs and authored examples are identified examples within that larger body.

Use direct, affirmative descriptions. Place material qualifiers beside the claims they delimit: original producer and date, synthetic workload, accounting unit, commitment term, proposed integration. Detailed protocol limits and failure handling belong in the relevant component reference. Public intros and footers explain the product or action rather than reciting internal approval arguments.

A current view can be edited without rewriting its historical sources. Preserve source captures, earlier measurement records, licenses, attribution, data-access controls and actual acceptance behavior. Present missing inputs as specific open fields or unavailable actions. Hashes identify declared artifacts; the interface labels measurements, source re-imports, recomputations and checked reuse according to what occurred.

Build generated pages through their owning templates and builders. The root overview and source README lead to the historical reader, current work and supported execution. A download should contain the same example its page describes. Source records remain inspectable after presentation changes.

The single Cantos CI combines the existing component checks with public-surface coverage, generated-page parity and historical-reader journeys. Pages deployment consumes that same source-exact result. Refer to `BUILD.md` for build commands and `site-surfaces.json` for the finite page inventory.
