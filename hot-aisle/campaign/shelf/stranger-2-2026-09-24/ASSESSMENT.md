The response is not a fully admitted card. Besides the observer relationship, its inspection
record lacks an actor and time while a source entry supplies a local inspection date. Its
relative `source.json` refers to `input/source.json`, as recorded in the handoff. These remain
visible in the raw output; the parent did not repair the filing and then grade the repaired copy.

The validator checks whether the required representation is present. It cannot establish that
a populated disclosure is complete, that an unknown was unavoidable, or that a claim is true.
This review supplies those separate judgments. One changed model, source artifact and set of
instructions cannot isolate why the two earlier errors disappeared. The result supports another
bounded handoff; it does not settle general usability or show that institutions or humans have
adopted the filing system.

The source is Voltage Park's [pricing page](https://www.voltagepark.com/pricing), captured at
the UTC time in `input/source.json` (September 24 in the operator's local time). The packet
contains selected excerpts, not an assertion that the full page has no publication metadata.
