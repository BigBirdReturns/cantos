# GPU Pricing Lane - Neocloud Providers (2026-09-24)

## Providers with Public Pricing

**Lambda** - H100, H200, B200 pricing available. Reserved 16-GPU clusters and on-demand single GPUs. On-demand B200: $6.69/hr, H100: $3.99/hr.

**Nebius** - Most comprehensive pricing. H100 $3.85-4.50/hr, H200 $4.50-5.40/hr, B200 $7.15-8.50/hr (Oct 1 increase scheduled). Two rows per GPU showing current and Oct 1 pricing.

**Crusoe** - H100 $3.90/hr, H200 $4.29/hr on-demand. MI300X $3.45/hr. B200, GB200 require sales contact. Billing by minute.

**Together AI** - H100 $3.99/hr on-demand, $3.19/hr reserved (181+ days). H200 $5.99/hr. B200 $8.19/hr. Preemptible from $1.99/hr.

**Verda** - H100 $3.45/hr on-demand, $1.73/hr spot. H200 $4.55/hr on-demand, $2.27/hr spot. B200 $6.75/hr on-demand, $3.38/hr spot. Reserved discounts 2-25% off.

**Massed Compute** - H100 $2.73/hr (cheapest H100 on-demand), H200 $3.62/hr. B200 $5.43/GPU/hr (8-GPU minimum). B300 $6.60/GPU/hr (8-GPU minimum). No spot pricing listed.

**Voltage Park** - H100 $1.99/hr starting price (cheapest listed single GPU). Cluster pricing above requires sales contact.

**Latitude.sh** - H100 $1.68/hr (lowest on-demand single GPU price found). Other GPUs not listed on this page.

**Scaleway** - Limited pricing available. H100 listed but rates not shown; need to select PAR-2 zone. L4 available at €0.79-6.30/hr depending on config. H200, B200, MI300X not listed.

## Providers - No Public Pricing

**SF Compute (sfcompute.com/pricing)** - Requires authentication/login to view pricing. Claims "market price for H100 hour" but price not accessible without auth.

**Prime Intellect (www.primeintellect.ai/pricing)** - HTTP 404 not found.

**FluidStack (www.fluidstack.io/pricing)** - HTTP 404 not found.

**Cudo Compute (www.cudocompute.com/pricing)** - Lists H100, H200, B200, GB200 but all pricing marked "Quote on request". No public rates.

**Hyperbolic** - Main pricing page 404. App redirect to hyperbolic.ai/compute mentions "unbeatable pay-as-you-go pricing" for H100/H200 but no actual rates listed. Refers to docs/general/billing-payments for details.

**OVHcloud** - Pricing page mentions H200, H100, L40S, L4 but no actual prices in public content. Requires direct navigation or sales contact.

## Summary

- 9 providers with usable on-demand pricing data (Lambda, Nebius, Crusoe, Together AI, Verda, Massed Compute, Voltage Park, Latitude.sh, Scaleway)
- 4 providers behind login or sales wall (SF Compute, Cudo Compute, OVHcloud, Hyperbolic)
- 2 providers with 404 errors (Prime Intellect, FluidStack)
- Best on-demand rates for single H100 GPU: Latitude.sh $1.68/hr, Voltage Park $1.99/hr, Massed Compute $2.73/hr
- Nebius has scheduled Oct 1 2026 price increases documented
- Most providers don't specify minimum billing or regions for single-GPU instances
- Spot pricing available from: Verda (50% discount), Together AI ($1.99-4.09/hr preemptible)
