## Selected-source mapping and limits

InferenceX retains richer workload/config dimensions, so it is the first comparison prior. MLPerf provides release-indexed checkpoints and system metadata, but its Server/Offline datasets and accuracy rules do not match our random-token cells. Unknown MLPerf lengths/concurrency deliberately block ratios.

InferenceX aggregate latency fields without `_ms` are seconds; per-GPU throughput already has a GPU denominator. The documented single-node physical count is TP × PP × PCP, not TP × EP × DCP. Split/multinode topology without an explicit physical count stays unknown. See the [aggregate contract](https://github.com/SemiAnalysisAI/InferenceX/blob/main/docs/results-and-ingestion.md).

MLPerf metric labels are checked against [LoadGen's producer](https://github.com/mlcommons/inference/blob/master/loadgen/results.cc). Samples/s stays samples/s; inferred tokens/s stays distinct from counted tokens/s. A VALID performance log does not prove accuracy/compliance passed. Specific live historical files, source commits, model revisions and runtime digests are **UNVERIFIED** in this build; no invented pins or copied marketing numbers are fixtures.

## Local artifact verification, Fix round 1

The initial UNVERIFIED/live-file limitation above is superseded for the 100 locally supplied InferenceX aggregates: 198 rows were inspected/imported, with 28 empty files retained in the manifest. Head SHAs and artifact timestamps come from the supplied index; model revisions and independent upstream verification remain UNVERIFIED. Five byte-identical files are now fixtures. Real single/multi-node records also supply num_gpus, num_aggregate_gpu, or explicit num_prefill_gpu/num_decode_gpu counts; SCHEMA.md defines precedence. Three zero-success agentic rows are retained, including one 0/0 row with undefined success rate. No network reads were performed in this fix round.
