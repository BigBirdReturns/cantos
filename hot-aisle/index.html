Equations

Break-even Q_hot / Q_other = C_hot / C_other

Price scope

Hot Aisle uses the public MI300X VM rate of $2.99/GPU-hour for new allocations and $3.39/GPU-hour for 8-GPU bare metal. Nebius uses public AI Cloud on-demand GPU prices. Customer quotes, spot pricing, storage, tax, managed-service fees and unentered extras are outside the model.

Evidence boundary

This page does not claim a GPU performance result. User-entered throughput remains unverified until backed by a reproducible run with pinned software, identical acceptance criteria and the full billed interval.
