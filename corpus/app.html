Cantos is the body of work; Second Run makes applicable work executable. This local collection connects discovery to retained evidence, the producing commits, runs and issues the capture recorded, and normalization attempts. It does not contain benchmark reruns, completed calibration runs, or producers' decision rationale.
Collection custody and portability

Source files remain in your collection. Attempts are retained separately as native Research Desk packets. Export them, retain different criteria, and continue with your own copy of the tools; this interface requires no account or remote service. Actor names are labels, not authenticated identities.
