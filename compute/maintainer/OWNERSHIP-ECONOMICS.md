# Source-bound ownership and reserved-capacity economics

Run `python3 -m compute.maintainer.ownership_economics CAMPAIGN --scenario compute/maintainer/examples/ownership-scenario.json` from the extracted maintainer kit. CAMPAIGN is the extracted `cantos-vllm-humaneval-evidence-20261118` directory from `evidence/vllm-humaneval-20261118.zip`. This command validates original receipt-hashed vLLM serving captures, raw board telemetry, task grades, trial times and accepted denominators before calculating any scenario costs. It makes no inference request.

The example JSON deliberately contains illustrative assumptions, not recorded invoices, power measurements, purchase prices or operator activity. Supply both arms' explicitly priced hardware, life in **calendar** hours, availability and utilization of available capacity, operations per reserved calendar hour, network/storage per captured active hour, non-board active watts and whole-host idle watts. Supply shared tariff, electricity overhead multiplier, verifier cost, after-failure recovery, operator time/rate and sample acceptance floor. All fields are mandatory, finite and domain-checked.

For each arm the model projects reserved capacity hours as captured active hours / (availability × utilization) and idle/unavailable hours as the residual. It distinguishes measured board energy from assumed non-board active energy and whole-host idle electricity. It charges hardware amortization and calendar operations across reserved capacity; verification against all graded attempts; assumed post-failure recovery only against observed failed attempts. Captured failures are already present in observed energy/time and never duplicated as simulated inference retries. Actual concurrency, idle power, facility overhead, availability, life, and supplier prices are not measured by the retained campaign.

The report preserves original source hashes, scenario hash and component costs. It reports conditional candidate-minus-baseline modeled USD per accepted application task and the break-even *candidate hardware acquisition price*, holding all other assumptions fixed. An arm that fails the explicitly declared application acceptance floor cannot win procurement on cheap cost; this campaign's 21/96 acceptance per arm yields `hold_both_below_observed_acceptance_floor` at the example floor 0.8. Even a passing sample is not automatically representative production or provider qualification.

Acquisition price is amortized over nominal calendar service life; availability and utilization determine what fraction of those calendar hours support the captured work. Idle power is assumed across both unavailable and unoccupied hours, which is a scenario simplification, not a measurement of outages. Operator charges represent **additional** post-failure recovery and exclude captured inference time. Change prices without new inference; changes to execution, hardware, workload, evaluation or energy sensor require new dependent observations.
