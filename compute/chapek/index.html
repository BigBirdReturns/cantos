      What the customer keeps
      Each accepted piece of work should leave whoever picks it up next ahead.
      Expensive reasoning and GPU work can produce a tested procedure and a usable record. The next suitable task can reuse them. New conditions expose the unresolved work rather than silently inheriting old conclusions.
      Customer-owned state
      Authoritative sources, revisions, permissions, accepted decisions, exceptions and unfinished obligations.
      Keep the record portable and the actual systems of record authoritative. Reconstructible indexes and caches remain secondary.
      Qualified procedures
      Inputs, applicability conditions, code, model and runtime pins where relevant, validation checks and known failure cases.
      A tested procedure can re-run a resolved check without a new model call. A saved rationale does not replace the model’s general capability.
      Accountable operation
      Chapek would maintain the agreed environment, qualified changes, recovery and support, while the customer keeps consequential authority.
      Model work runs on Chapek capacity by default. Retained procedures make each later run quicker to check, so the customer gets more accepted work from the same server.
      One workflow, continued
      01Recover the current case. Open the relevant sources, prior decisions and their conditions.
      02Identify the changed dependency. Keep unaffected work usable and expose the unresolved part.
      03Perform the authorized next step. Use a qualified procedure or approved model work on the customer’s capacity.
      04Check and review. Retain the actual result, failure, reviewer’s rationale and remaining limits.
      05Hand over. A successor continues with the sources, procedure and working state rather than reconstructing a chat.
      This page performs the decision and handover parts in your browser. It does not run this engineering workflow or connect to a model or GPU.
