# Cantos Research Desk — bounded architecture

## Product and authority

Cantos is the umbrella for work that can be continued from its sources. Second Run is the execution and reuse program. This page is one research-to-decision aperture. Its reusable sequence is **source and claim → measurement → economics → recommendation → delivery → changed input → revised recommendation**. Ben is a prospective viewer; this build establishes no client adoption or endorsement.

The page consumes a public-safe Run 3 case, uses a generated copy of the existing ResearchCore for records, dependency pins, local review, immutable freeze and packet verification, and projects economic calculations from the existing arithmetic owner. The original `research-desk/app.html` remains byte-identical. The browser does not grade code, rent a GPU, seek a live quote, authenticate a reviewer, sign a shard or publish a report. These are separate owners and effects.

## The eight-record case

`data/seed.json` contains Run 3 and disclosure source records, separate A/T0 and N/T0 measurement claims, the A/T0 price source, one Compute-derived A/T0 economic claim, a separately scoped N/T0 accounting context, and one recommendation. Prices and measured results are distinct inputs. If the A/T0 rate is changed as a scenario, the price source receives a new revision. The pinned `economics-a` and `recommendation` then become stale while both measurements remain current. A successor economic claim and recommendation pin the new price revision. A current accepted local review is required for each issued snapshot; earlier snapshots remain historical. Export carries the journal, and restore verifies it before replacing local work.

The scenario is a controlled intervention on an assumption, never a provider quote or a new observation of capacity, quality or latency. At an illustrative $5.00/GPU-hour for A/T0, the same retained 4,336 accepted and 64.7-minute modeled own-seat equivalent yield about $1.243 per 1,000 accepted, versus N/T0's historical $1.20 at its separate closed-ledger boundary. This reverses the arithmetic ordering under the explicitly named boundaries. It does not warrant a purchase or claim identical invoice classes.

## Public-safe transfer

The distributable allowlist is the case's dated numbers, scoped claim language, relative source coordinates, SHA-256 identities, the generated application projection, and user-entered local review/history. Raw task completions, source text beyond the bounded summary, internal accounts, seat/host names, private strategy and absolute estate paths are excluded. A user export may contain their own entered text, so the user controls that packet. Hash consistency and local review do not prove that an upstream claim is true or that the reviewer is who they say they are.
