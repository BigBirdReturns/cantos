# Open Compute delivery and acceptance

Cantos connects evidence, comparison and retained decisions. SecondRun supplies execution, dependency tracking and valid reuse. The Open Compute outcome spans inference and application outcomes, authorized operational audits, operating economics and independent recipient use. The public developer workflow is one explicit acceptance path within that outcome.

## Completion means an operable measurement system

A recipient can identify a baseline and candidate, specify a representative workload and requirements, execute or import source-bound measurements, inspect failures and assumptions, compare eligible configurations, retain the decision and update it when inputs change. Every result carries its producer, observation time, conditions and evidence class. Existing measurements and attributed third-party evidence remain useful inputs. New execution answers changed conditions or material unanswered questions.

A maintainer can run a fixed workload against baseline and candidate serving configurations; capture streamed responses, timing, exact acceptance and failures; compare repeated trials with the intended changed factor recorded; apply explicit thresholds; and receive a readable report plus a CI verdict. Source lives in `compute/maintainer/`; retained execution integrates through `integration/work.py` and `integration/WORK.md`.

A price-only change may reuse observations and recompute economics. A serving implementation, model, workload, protocol or hardware change invalidates affected observations. An explicit fresh request executes even for a previously seen specification. Reuse preserves the original observation date. Trace reanalysis, traffic replay and evaluated application success retain distinct meanings.

## Evidence required by scope

| Scope | Required behavior and evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Inference and useful work | Source-bound requests and model/runtime/tokenizer/precision/configuration identity; streaming and end-to-end timing; concurrency; token accounting; failures/retries; independently checkable application outcomes; meaningful runtime/accelerator comparisons under stated conditions. |
| Operations | Executable acquisition/evaluation for authorized performance, reliability/recovery, network, storage, lifecycle, security, monitoring, pricing and availability. Local observations, published provider claims and simulated faults retain their actual scopes. |
| Economics | Accepted work and failures joined to attributable energy, utilization, ownership and operating assumptions, reliability, retry/verification/operator/idle costs, cost per accepted task and sensitivity. Board telemetry remains board telemetry; modeled prices remain assumptions. |
| Recipient use | Runnable public source and commands, licenses/source manifests, representative raw evidence, exact supported scope, comparison and retained history/export, with release verification tied to the delivered bytes. |
| Maintainer adoption path | Independent recipient uses the baseline/candidate CLI and SecondRun integration to obtain valid pass/regression/incomparable/error outcomes, preserve raw evidence, and demonstrate changed-input invalidation and explicit fresh execution. |

## Reporting a completed result

Name the capability delivered, exact source and artifacts, observed recipient behavior, and remaining scope. A passing build, HTTP response, archive, small fixture or published site proves its stated property. The top-level outcome closes only when its scope-specific evidence supports that conclusion. No claim of competitor displacement or customer adoption follows automatically from implementation.
